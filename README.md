# Phase Reveal

A team submission for [Global Game Jam](https://globalgamejam.org) 2026 under the theme "Mask", made using [Godot](https://godotengine.org). This project has its own [itch.io](https://avaxar.itch.io/phase-reveal) page.

## Description

A side-scrolling puzzle platformer with an [exclusive-or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exclusive_or) twist. Various platforms exist in multiple additively-colorful planes of existence: red, green, and blue. You are a laboratory monkey whose bestowed mask enables you to selectively and intricately interact with them. Two platforms of contrasting hues ***mask*** each other out, becoming zilch in mutual intersection. Escape the facility, phasing your way out.

## Controls

The movement controls are as what you would expect.

- **A** or **←** to move leftwards.
- **D** or **→** to move rightwards.
- **Space** or **W** or **↑** to jump.
- **E** to pick up and place down weight cubes.

The numbers 1-3 allow you to toggle your three masks.

1. Toggle the **red** mask.
2. Toggle the **green** mask.
3. Toggle the **blue** mask.

Press Esc to pause the game and take a break.

## Objectives

- Move weight cubes over onto the pressure buttons to open gates.
- Will platforms in and out of existence using your respective masks.
- Be careful or opportunistic of ***mask-phasing*** through two intersecting platforms.
- Not fall into the sharp deadly spikes.
- Not let yourself or your cubes be crushed whilst carelessly toggling masks.
- Phase your way out for forestry freedom!
